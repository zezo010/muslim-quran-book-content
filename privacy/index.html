التطبيق مجاني وغير ربحي: لا إعلانات فيه، ولا اشتراكات، ولا مشتريات، ولا حسابات تسجيل. لا نجمع أي معلومة تدل على شخصك، ولا نبيع بيانات ولا نشاركها مع أحد.

ما يبقى على جهازك فقط

  • إعداداتك (اللغة والمظهر والقارئ والتنبيهات)، وموضع التوقف، والآيات المفضلة، وعدّادات الأذكار والتسبيح: تُحفظ على جهازك. وإذا كان النسخ الاحتياطي في جهازك مفعّلًا، فقد يحفظ نظام أندرويد هذه البيانات وحدها ضمن نسختك الاحتياطية في حساب Google لتعود معك إلى جهاز جديد.
  • التلاوات والتفاسير والترجمات التي تحمّلها: تُحفظ على جهازك، ويمكنك حذفها من التطبيق.

Privacy Policy — “Muslim Quran book”

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Developer: HexaGen AI · App: Muslim Quran book (com.hexagen.ai.muslim.quran.book)

The app is free and non-profit: no ads, no subscriptions, no purchases, no sign-in. We do not collect anything that identifies you, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (language, appearance, reciter, alerts), your stop position, favourite verses, and adhkar and tasbih counters are stored on your device. If backup is on in your phone, Android may keep this data — and only this data — in your Google account backup so it returns on a new phone.
  • Recitations, tafsirs and translations you download are stored on your device; you can delete them in the app.

Location

The app asks for approximate location only, and only when you tap “Use my location” or “Use my phone’s location”, to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction on your device. If you have allowed it, the Qibla screen reads your approximate location once when it opens, to point from where you are, and does not keep it. Your location is never sent to any server and is not used in the background. You can instead pick your city from a list stored in the app, with no permission at all.

Notifications and alarms

Azan and reminder alerts are scheduled on your device only; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Crash reports

If the app stops because of an error, it sends a technical report to Google’s Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it: the error trace, the device model and Android version, the app version, and a random identifier for the app installation that does not identify you. Reports do not include your name, email, location or advertising ID. Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy terms.

Internet use

The app connects to these sites only to download content you ask for: everyayah.com (recitations), quranenc.com (approved tafsirs and translations) and raw.githubusercontent.com (tafsir files we publish). As with any download, the server sees your device’s IP address; we send nothing else with it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from any user, children included, and shows no ads.
